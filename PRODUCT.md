# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-selected plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in one index.html, without a build step.

## Users and purpose
Viewers arriving at the end of withfouad's YouTube video “Mastering Codex.” Help them revisit the six parts, keep a cheat sheet, and find the creator's social channels. Success means a complete, polished phone experience.

## Capabilities and constraints
Six parts: Foundation, Environment, Git, Models and tokens, Control and skills, Tools and scale. Two or three takeaways per part. Downloadable cheat sheet. YouTube, Telegram, and Instagram placeholder links explicitly requested by the user. No transcript or account URLs supplied; takeaways are authored companion notes, not quotes from the video.

## Brand commitments
withfouad; background #030303, white text, sole red accent #D10F0F; Inter Tight and Instrument Serif. Preserve the project AGENTS.md.
